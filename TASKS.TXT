# SANKALP AI — World-Class Frontend Prompts
###  Design Reference: sarvam.ai aesthetic — clean, purposeful, Indian-context-aware
### Stack: React + Next.js + Tailwind + Framer Motion + Recharts + Socket.IO

---

> **MASTER DESIGN BRIEF (prepend to every prompt)**
>
> Design language: Clean Indian govtech meets modern SaaS. Think sarvam.ai — restrained white space, sharp typography, purposeful color, data-forward layouts. NOT a government portal. NOT a generic dashboard. This is an intelligence platform. The visual identity should feel like something the Prime Minister's office would proudly demo.
>
> **Color System:**
> - Primary: `#0F2D5E` (Deep Navy — authority, trust)
> - Accent: `#FF6B2B` (Saffron-Orange — Indian identity, urgency, action)
> - Success: `#16A34A`
> - Warning: `#F59E0B`
> - Danger: `#DC2626`
> - Background: `#F8F9FC` (near-white, slightly cool)
> - Surface: `#FFFFFF`
> - Text Primary: `#0A0F1E`
> - Text Secondary: `#6B7280`
> - Border: `#E5E7EB`
>
> **Typography:**
> - Display/Headings: `Sora` (geometric, modern, confident)
> - Body: `DM Sans` (legible, neutral, humanist)
> - Data/Mono: `JetBrains Mono` (dashboards, IDs, codes)
> - Hindi text: `Noto Sans Devanagari`
>
> **Principles:**
> - Every screen must look credible to a government bureaucrat AND impressive to a VC
> - Real data labels everywhere (use Delhi ward names, real complaint categories)
> - Micro-interactions on every interactive element
> - Mobile-first, but desktop is the hero presentation surface
> - NO purple gradients. NO generic startup purple. NO Inter font.
